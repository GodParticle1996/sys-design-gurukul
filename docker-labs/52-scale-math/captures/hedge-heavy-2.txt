MODEL: seeded simulation (seed 52), two servers, one worker and one FIFO line each, 1,00,000 requests per row
normal work: random, avg 8 ms
stragglers: 1% of requests are heavy and take 200 ms on either server (same request, same slowness)
hedge wait = p95 of the no-hedge row at the same load; budget = at most 5% of requests get a spare copy
cancel = a spare copy still waiting in line is dropped once the other copy answers (a running copy is not stopped)
copies sent = spare copies sent; copies run = spare copies that started; extra ms = service time of extra copies ÷ first copies
