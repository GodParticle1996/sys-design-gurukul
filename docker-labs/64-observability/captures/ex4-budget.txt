   (budget = poora band rehne ke minute, ya utne hi "poore fail minute" ke barabar errors)
   SLO 99.90% → 43.20 min
   SLO 99.95% → 21.60 min
   SLO 99.99% → 4.32 min

   Requests mein bhi gin sakte ho: 10 crore requests/mahina, 99.9% → 1,00,000 fail ho sakti hain.
