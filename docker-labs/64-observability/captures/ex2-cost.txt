ex2 — kaunsa signal kitna mehenga (hisaab, maane hue numbers ke saath; sampling ek seeded model)

1) Ek din ka data — 10 lakh requests/minute (video ka "millions per minute"), har request ke logs ~1 KB (maana):
   logs:    1.44 TB/din  (30 din rakho toh 43.2 TB)
   metrics: kul 300 series × har 15 s ek sample × ~2 byte (Prometheus docs: disk par compressed 1–2 byte/sample) = 3.5 MB/din
            (sirf samples ka disk; index, WAL aur RAM mein chalti series ka hisaab alag — RAM ka kharcha series ki ginti se badhta hai)
   → in maanyataon par ~4 lakh× ka farq (logs ka compression nahi joda; 10× dabaane par bhi ~42 hazaar×).
     Video ka "saalon ke metrics memory mein" — disk par sasta, RAM ka kharcha active series par tika.
     Metric sasta isliye ki metric ginti hai, kahani nahi.

2) Metric tab tak sasta jab tak labels ki ginti chhoti hai (har label-combination = ek alag series):
   http_requests_total{method, route, status}            → zyada se zyada 300 series
   + user_id label (10 lakh users)                        → zyada se zyada 30,00,00,000 series (30 crore)
   → "metric sasta hai" tabhi tak sach hai. Prometheus docs: user ID jaise anginat values label mein mat daalo.
     "Kis user ka fail hua" — sawaal logs/traces ka hai.

3) Traces: 1,00,000 requests, 0.1% error, 1% dheemi; dono ka sikka 1% (seeded model):
   tareeka                                    rakhe     errors rakhe     dheemi rakhe
   sab                                        1,00,000            108          1,006
   head (shuru mein sikka uchhaalo, 1%)          1,027              0             12
   tail (khatam hone par: saare error/dheeme     2,126            108          1,006
         + baaki ka 1%)
   head, 3 aur seeds par (errors rakhe / kul errors): 1 / 97 · 1 / 94 · 0 / 115
   → head sampling sasta hai par talaash wali request (error, dheemi) aksar phenk deta hai.
     Tail sampling (is aadarsh model mein) har error rakhta hai — shart: poora trace samay par collector tak pahunche;
     der se aaya error span pehle liye faisle ko nahi badal sakta. Faisle se pehle saare spans bhejne aur rakhne ka kharcha bhi lagta hai.
     Keemat: ek trace ke saare spans ek hi collector tak aane chahiye, aur collector faisle tak (OpenTelemetry default 30 s)
     poora trace memory mein rakhta hai.
