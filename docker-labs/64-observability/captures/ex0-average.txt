1) Video ka misaal: 100 requests — 99 × 10 ms, 1 × 10 s
   1 dheema: average 109.9 ms · p50 10 ms · p90 10 ms · p99 (nearest-rank) 10 ms · p99 (linear) 109.9 ms · max 10 s
   2 dheema: average 209.8 ms · p50 10 ms · p90 10 ms · p99 (nearest-rank) 10 s · p99 (linear) 10 s · max 10 s
   → 1 dheema request par nearest-rank p99 = 10 ms: dheema request p99 se UPAR baitha hai. p99 "sabse bure 1%" ki
     seema hai, unka haal nahi. Tool ki percentile definition bhi number badal deti hai (10 ms vs 109.9 ms).
     Isliye max (ya p99.9) aur dheeme requests ki ginti bhi dekho.

2) Ek user, kai requests (har request 1% chance se dheemi, ek-doosre se aazaad — maana hua)
   requests/user   kam se kam ek dheemi
               1   1.0%

              50   39.5%
             100   63.4%
   → percentile requests ka hai, users ka nahi. 100 requests wale session mein 63.4% users kam se kam ek dheemi request dekhte hain.

3) Teen hosts, ek dashboard: "har host ka p99, phir unka average" vs asli p99 (seeded model, 10,000 requests)
   chhota-host-bimar: A (9,000 req) p99 24.1 ms · B (900 req) p99 25.6 ms · C (100 req) p99 874.7 ms
      p99 ka average 308.1 ms · asli p99 (saare requests) 26.0 ms · histogram jod kar 33.9 ms (bucket 25–50 ms)
   bada-host-bimar: A (9,000 req) p99 301.4 ms · B (900 req) p99 23.5 ms · C (100 req) p99 24.5 ms
      p99 ka average 116.5 ms · asli p99 (saare requests) 299.0 ms · histogram jod kar 369.1 ms (bucket 250–500 ms)
   → p99 ka average koi percentile nahi: ek case mein 11.8× zyada bataya, doosre mein 2.6× kam.
     Histogram ke buckets jod sakte ho (ginti judti hai), phir p99 nikaalo — jawab bucket ki chaudai jitna sahi,
     jab tak jawab kisi finite bucket mein ho. Sabse upar (+Inf, is lab mein 10 s se upar) gira toh sirf 10 s ki seema milti hai:
     teen 20 s ki requests ka histogram p99 = 10 s. Badi latency ke liye bada aakhri bucket rakho.
