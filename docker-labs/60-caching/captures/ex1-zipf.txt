→ "90% requests 10% data par" ek kanoon nahi: s = 0.99 par bhi top 10% ko 80%, s = 0.6 par 39%. Sab barabar ho toh
  10% cache = 10% hits. LRU ka 10% cache poore top 10% (jo hamesha rakha jaaye) se thoda kam pakadta hai, kyunki
  kam-popular keys bhi aakar jagah gherti hain. Apne asli logs se skew naapo, phir cache ka size chuno.
