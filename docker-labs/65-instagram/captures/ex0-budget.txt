   Uploads/din (50 crore DAU × 1/5)       10 crore           10,00,00,000
   Uploads/second (average)               ~1,150             1,157
   Uploads/second (peak, ×3)              3,500              3,472
   Naya storage/din (× 3 MB)              300 TB             300 TB
   Naya storage/saal                      110 PB             109.5 PB
   Photo views/second (× 200/din)         1.2 million        11,57,407
   Egress (× 200 KB)                      240 GB/s, 1.9 Tbps 231.5 GB/s, 1.85 Tbps
   Read : write                           1,000 : 1          1,000 : 1
   Likes/second (× 10/din), peak ×3       57,000 · 170,000   57,870 · 1,73,611
   → hisaab sahi. Likes photo uploads se ~50× zyada writes: asli write load chhota sa "like" hai.

2) Egress ka bill (list price $0.05/GB — maana; itne bade paimaane par asli daam contract ya apne network par tike):
   20 PB/din × $0.05/GB = $10,00,000/din. Images 30% chhoti → $3,00,000/din bachat.
   → storage company nahi, delivery company. Isliye CDN aur ISP ke andar cache.

3) Photo database ke andar rakho toh (Postgres):
   WAL mein likhna: 1,157 uploads/s × 5 MB = 5.8 GB/s average (peak ×3 = 17.4 GB/s). Video: "6 GB/s" — sahi.
   5 MB photo = 2,506 TOAST chunks (har chunk 1,996 byte, 8 KB page).
   Sirf metadata (150 byte row): 1 GiB mein 71,58,278 rows; Postgres ke 27 byte tuple header/pointer ke saath ~60,66,337.
   Video ka "1 GB mein 1 crore rows" thoda zyada; mudda sahi — photo bahar (object storage), DB mein sirf URL.

4) 110 PB ko bachaana (lab 14 asli Reed-Solomon chalata hai):
   3 copies: 330 PB disk · erasure coding 10 data + 4 parity: 154 PB disk (1.4×), koi bhi 4 fragment kho sakte ho.
   Keemat: ek fragment dobara banane ke liye 10 bache hue fragments network se padhne padte hain (copy wale mein 1).

5) New York ↔ Tokyo (~10,850 km), fibre mein roshni ~2 lakh km/s:
   kam se kam 54.3 ms ek taraf, 108.5 ms round trip. Asli raasta lamba: ~180 ms (video, maana).
   Pehle byte se pehle handshakes          seedha NY (180 ms RTT)    paas ka edge POP (15 ms RTT)
   TCP + TLS 1.2                            540 ms                    45 ms
   TCP + TLS 1.3                            360 ms                    30 ms
   QUIC (HTTP/3)                            180 ms                    15 ms
   → edge POP handshakes paas mein kar leta hai; data phir bhi warm connection par samundar paar jaata hai (ek round trip).
     Video: TLS 1.2 = 2 round trip — sahi; TCP ke saath kul 3.
