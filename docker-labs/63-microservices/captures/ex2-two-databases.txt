ASLI ginti (lib/twodb.js): "1 cheez khareedo" (operation id op1) = stock − 1 AUR order banao. Har step ke baad crash,
restart aur recovery; recovery khud bhi ek baar crash hokar dobara chalti hai. Har step ke baad ek reader dono databases ki
committed haalat padhta hai. Participants operation id ki receipt rakhte hain (dohraaya step dobara asar nahi karta).

tareeka                            cases   ant mein galat   reader ne beech ki haalat dekhi   crash par locks atke   recovery crash ke saath galat
ek DB, ek transaction                 4            0                        0                    0             0 of 9
saga, progress sirf memory mein       3            2                        3                    0             6 of 6
saga + durable saga log               5            0                        5                    0             0 of 12
2PC (do-phase commit)                 5            0                        3                    4             0 of 12

(Order fail ho toh: 2PC coordinator zinda hai toh turant abort — locks sirf 1 crash case mein atke.)

→ Ek database: ek ACID transaction — reader ko ya purani haalat dikhti hai ya poori nayi; crash par rollback.
→ Saga, progress sirf memory mein: stock ghata, order se pehle crash = stock gaya, order nahi, aur kisi ko yaad nahi.
→ Saga + durable log + receipts: recovery log padh kar idempotent steps dobara chalati hai — ant mein sahi, recovery khud crash
  ho tab bhi. Par har run mein reader ne "stock ghata, order nahi" dekha: saga mein isolation nahi.
→ 2PC: video kehta hai "do databases par transaction bilkul nahi". Hota hai (2PC/XA): ant mein kabhi galat nahi. Par (1) commit
  dono databases par alag pal mein lagta hai, toh alag-alag padhne wala reader beech ki haalat dekh sakta hai; (2) coordinator
  ne COMMIT log kar liya ho tab bhi, jab tak faisla participant tak na pahunche, participant locks pakad kar intezaar karta hai.
  Recovery: coordinator lautkar log padhta hai — COMMIT mila toh poora karo, nahi mila toh abort (presumed abort).
