Arithmetic: monolith ko copies se scale karo. Har copy mein 0.5 GB ka code jo zyadatar idle (video ka "user profile"),
aur har copy ka apna DB connection pool (max 20). Postgres ka max_connections aam taur par 100 (kuch slots reserved).

copies   idle RAM (sab copies)   max DB connections (har pool bhar jaaye toh)   Postgres default (100) ke mukable
     2               1 GB                                40                     0.4×
    10               5 GB                               200                     2.0×
    50              25 GB                             1,000                    10.0×
   100              50 GB                             2,000                    20.0×

Split karke: image service ki 100 copies (DB nahi chahiye) + baaki app ki 4 copies → max DB connections 80.

→ Video sahi: idle RAM aksar sasta hai (100 copies × 0.5 GB = 50 GB). Par copies sirf RAM nahi, DB connections bhi guna
  karti hain: busy samay mein har pool bhar sakta hai — 100 × 20 = 2,000, default se 20 guna (pool khaali shuru hote hain,
  zaroorat par bharte hain). Ilaaj monolith mein bhi hai: chhota pool, PgBouncer jaisa
  connection pooler, ya bhaari kaam (image) ko alag worker process mein — poori microservice ki zaroorat nahi. Lab 16 ex2 asli
  monolith copies dikhata hai.
